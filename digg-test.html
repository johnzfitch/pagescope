We're Officially digg.com

Hope everyone had a Happy New Year!

In preparation for the next phase of Digg, we made the transition from beta.digg.com to digg.com. We’re still in private beta, but as part of some work internally we’ve decided to move to our official domain.

Now, if you visit beta.digg.com, you will be redirected to digg.com.

As part of this transition you will need to re-authenticate, simply because cookies don’t play well across domains. After that things will essentially work as normal.

We’re looking forward to all that is on the horizon this year, and even more excited to do it with you all. As always if you have any issues feel free to @ mention me directly.
